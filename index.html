Our Story

Started on one chair in 2016.

Blackline Barber opened on Dorp Street with a single chair and a simple idea: a proper barbershop for Cape Town, where the cut is unhurried and the line-up is exact. Ten years on, we've kept the same standard — three barbers, one shop, no rush.

We named it Blackline for the thing every good barber is judged on: a clean, confident line, whether that's a fade, a beard edge or a straight part. Everything we do comes back to that line.

Terms & Conditions

1. Bookings

All appointments booked through this website or by phone are held for the client until 10 minutes past the scheduled start time. Arrivals after this window may be treated as a no-show and asked to rebook, at the barber's discretion, depending on the day's schedule.

2. Cancellations & rescheduling

We ask for at least 4 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This allows us to offer the slot to another client. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may require a deposit for future bookings.

3. Pricing

Prices listed on this website are current at time of publishing and are subject to change without prior notice. The price confirmed at the time of your appointment is the price payable, not necessarily the price shown online at a later date.

4. Payment

Payment is due at the end of your appointment. We accept cash and major card payments in-store. Online bookings do not require upfront payment.

5. Minors

Clients under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the duration of the appointment.

6. Health & hygiene

All tools are sanitised between every client in line with standard barbering hygiene practice. Please inform your barber of any skin conditions, allergies or sensitivities before your service begins.

7. Right to refuse service

Blackline Barber reserves the right to refuse or discontinue service to any client behaving in a manner that is abusive, unsafe, or disruptive to staff or other clients.

8. Liability

While every care is taken with each service, Blackline Barber is not liable for reactions to products where a client has not disclosed a known allergy or sensitivity in advance.

9. Website use

This website and its booking tool are provided for the purpose of browsing services and scheduling appointments. Content, branding and imagery on this site remain the property of Blackline Barber.

10. Governing law
